A review of coffee shops and coffee resources.

coffee ideas

  • acidity is interesting; darker roasts drop acidity which is NOT a bad thing. I actually love a tea-like low acidity cup with a silky body (achieved with a slightly darker roast)
  • I do not like acidic espressos (light roast or extra light roasts); espressos amplify flavor and make it quite sour
  • For pourovers, I expected to like all fruity cups but learned I like them in light bodied coffees; I had a heavier bodied red-fruit cup that became a bit too wine-like and naseating to drink

coffee recipes

https://sca.coffee/store

  • the flavor wheel here is quite useful; I wish I tabulated all the coffees I have tasted
  • the cupping attributes is quite nice when thinking about how to formulate opinions on coffee taste as well
  • I see this in a lot of shops (not many great ones though...)

https://www.baristahustle.com/coffee-compass/

  • I have seen this in a few shops
  • I am upset it removes a few dimensions: temperature, pouring speed/structure
  • temperature affects the body and if coffee is heavily processed, metalic flavors creep in and make the cup terrible
  • Besides that, I think it is useful to remind yourself about what you can do

https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLzDmvPifPhgpXKH4OVL5JhXzKgEsOCrhD

  • april coffee recipes are great; I prefer the 2-pour method when possible
  • doesn't post often

https://www.youtube.com/@yukiscoffeeuniversity/videos

  • Yuki's cofee university is a great resource for all kinds of coffee drinks
  • he has great attention to detail and doesn't waste words on meaningless chatter
  • doesn't post often

  • he has a great video on pourover blooming and extraction; this is one of my favorites
