iced pourovers

2026-09-19

A good iced pourover recipe.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PApBycDrPo0

James Hoffman has a pretty good iced pourover recipe here.

  • 2:3 ratio of ice to water
  • 2-3x water to coffee grounds in bloom (45s bloom)

I used 32.5g coffee, 200g ice, 300ml of water (split into 90ml first pour, 45s bloom + light stir, 210ml finish).

Coffee came out a bit more chocolatey than I hoped for and a touch bitter. I suspect the bloom was not great given the amount of coffee needed to cover in a short interval (bed looked uneven). The ice-to-water ratio is on point and the mouthfeel was correct but the taste was off (my fault). I think course grinds + higher temps would be interesting because initial ice did not fully dissolve after the two pours + shaking.

Next Time

  • Cut everything in half for more experiments and iterations: 16g coffee, 100g ice, 150ml water (32-48ml first pour) and a touch less agitation and higher temps
